Customs & Safety

Allowed vs Prohibited Items Guide

Ensure smooth international customs clearance by reviewing allowed items before packing.

Allowed Items (We Pack & Ship)

  • Homemade Sweets, Snacks, Namkeen & Bakery
  • Pickles (well-sealed non-leaking glass/plastic containers)
  • Spices, Masalas, Pulses, Rice & Dry Fruits
  • Clothes, Shoes, Bedding, Handicrafts & Books
  • Prescribed Medicines (with doctor's bill & prescription)
  • Educational Documents, Transcripts & Passports
  • Brass Utensils, Household Items & Gifts

Prohibited Items (Restricted by Air Cargo)

  • Flammable liquids, perfumes, spray deodorants, alcohol
  • Cash, currency notes, gold bullion, precious jewels
  • Loose Lithium-ion power banks or batteries
  • Explosives, fireworks, matches, lighters
  • Illegal substances, narcotics, unauthorized drugs
  • Perishable meat, unsealed liquid dairy items
